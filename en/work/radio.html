Challenge

Campus radio's biggest enemy is “nobody's listening.” We wanted a show students would seek out, not homework they had to sit through. Ghost stories were the hook, but the real question was how to hold attention with sound alone.

What I did

  • Helped develop the concept and positioning, creating a show that blends ghost stories with campus life and building its distinctive style and structure.
  • Wrote half of the scripts, collecting, adapting and creating stories to keep every episode tense and suspenseful.
  • Co-hosted the show, using delivery and emotional control to set the mood and connect with listeners.
  • Worked on post-production and promotion, including sound effects, music selection and social media strategy.
  • Also produced feature reports independently in the news department and led the weekly news-reading team.

Takeaway

Radio is the root of all my content skills. The pacing, build-up and emotional control I learned in a sound-only medium carry into short videos, posts and even pitch decks. It also taught me that good content is designed, not something that arrives when inspiration strikes.
