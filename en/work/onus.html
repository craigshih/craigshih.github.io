Challenge

A Hong Kong e-voucher fintech start-up wanted to enter Taiwan, but had no awareness, no case studies and no local content there. As the company's first person dedicated to the Taiwan market, I was building its local presence from a blank page.

What I did

  • Social: planned and designed assets, wrote posts, and set LinkedIn direction and scheduling for June–August.
  • Website: mapped the sitemap logic and designed the interaction architecture in Figma, delivering a working demo; introduced AEO to improve visibility in AI search engines.
  • Outbound: produced Taiwan-industry EDM assets and helped send them to 200+ companies.
  • Content: wrote 3–4 Chinese and English fintech blog posts on Taiwan market insights, published on the company site with SEO / AEO keyword work.
  • Research: joined the Hong Kong team on the annual English-language white paper, pulling internal campaign data and using pivot analysis to break down e-voucher redemption cycles and regional trends.

Takeaway

This was my first time running the full chain from fintech product to website experience to business conversion, and my first English-language industry research. Moving from execution-side communication to a research perspective was harder than I expected, and it's where I grew the most.
