Challenge

Amazon Global Selling Taiwan's social accounts had followers but no warmth; post views were stuck in the low hundreds. Meanwhile the seller community was flooded with the same basic questions every day, more than staff could answer. Both problems had the same root: the content wasn't aimed at what sellers were actually thinking about.

What I did

  • Ran content planning and publishing across FB / IG / Threads, rewriting posts from “official announcements” into entrepreneur stories told from the seller's point of view.
  • Created and produced content, developed educational materials, and worked closely on data analysis and cross-team collaboration.
  • Built and launched a simple HTML website on my own to bring brand information together in one place.
  • Connected chatbots (ManyChat, Crescendo Lab) to close the “social inquiry → need identification → information delivery” loop.
  • Managed the Amazon seller LINE community, consolidating and standardizing an industry FAQ library.

Takeaway

Growth doesn't come from posting more; it comes from posting the right thing. The turning point that doubled our numbers was switching the protagonist of each post from “Amazon the platform” to “someone hesitating over whether to start.” Automation follows the same logic: the bot isn't there to reduce replies, but to free people up for questions that need real judgment.
