Challenge

The 3rd Bm7 Songwriting Contest had 45 slots to fill, sponsors to land and a final to run within two summer months, and revenue had to cover costs. The hard part: it was a brand-new contest account with no followers and no track record. We had to convince amateur songwriters to enter, companies to pay and artist mentors to judge.

What I did

  • Contest social: ran the contest's Instagram from scratch. I conceived the planning, scripts, shooting and editing of the short videos, publishing 40+ posts and stories.
  • Sponsorship: secured 8 sponsors during preparation, worth over NT$30,000 in resources at market value; afterwards I proactively wrote a wrap-up report to protect both sides and show results.
  • Judges: negotiated with artist mentors to judge the final, sharpening my external communication and negotiation.
  • Event system: designed the Accupass event page, layout and content, and ran the platform's ads.
  • On-site: handled setup, crisis management and contestant communication on finals day, and shot and color-graded the performance photos.
  • Brand side: scripted and shot short videos for Being Music's official account, and wrote educational, promotional and conversion posts, including the pink-cable product push and the “Guitar Care Clinic” series.

Takeaway

This was the first time I ran an event's full business model: revenue has to cover costs, sponsors need a return, and contestants need to feel it was worth it. I proposed the wrap-up report myself, because I realized sponsors care about more than impressions. They want someone to turn the numbers into something they can report upward. Turning results into evidence people can use became my default on every project.
