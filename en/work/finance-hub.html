Challenge

Over time I'd written several finance automation programs on GitHub: a morning macro news digest, weekly fundamental research on Taiwan and US stocks, brokerage statement reconciliation, and price updates for my Notion budget. Each ran on its own and sent its own emails, so information was scattered across four places. I wanted one entry point that showed what I needed to know today, and that I could share with friends without exposing my holdings.

What I did

  • Worked with AI as the product owner: defined the problem each page solves and made the architecture calls and trade-offs. The AI wrote the code; I reviewed it and steered the direction.
  • Merged the four projects' output into one site that GitHub Actions rebuilds and deploys to Cloudflare every day, with no servers to maintain.
  • Designed “Today's Watch”: transparent rules based on institutional fund flows and sector trends pick one Taiwan and one US stock each day, and four simulated research personas write takes from fund-flow, trend, valuation and risk angles. It is deliberately framed as research material, not buy or sell advice.
  • Automatically reconciled my Notion records against brokerage statements stock by stock: holdings computed from 15 months of trades matched Notion exactly.
  • Handled privacy for sharing: the site uses email-code login, the personal investment page adds a server-side password, and I built a paper-trading game where friends compete with virtual money.
  • Iterated on the experience: turned 100,000-character research reports into tables and cards, and added sparklines, live news and animation so the key points land at a glance.

Takeaway

AI can write code, but what to build, how far to take it and where not to compromise are still human decisions. The key calls in this project weren't technical: whether to lock the investment page, whether the daily pick should be called a “recommendation” or a “watch,” and when reconciliation flagged 123 mismatches, whether to suspect the data or the code first. Stating requirements clearly and reviewing every step is the most important skill in working with AI.
