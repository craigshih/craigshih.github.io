Challenge

The firm's clients ranged from a traditional bank to a crypto exchange and an AI start-up: three completely different regulatory environments, audience mindsets and communication taboos. A message that's safe for a bank could be non-compliant for an exchange and too conservative for a start-up.

What I did

  • Bank SinoPac: designed a credit-card market strategy, analyzing target customers' spending behavior and preferences to propose a differentiated positioning.
  • HoyaBit crypto exchange: contributed to the brand communication plan, helping convey security and professionalism to Taiwanese investors within regulatory compliance.
  • ADGo: built the APAC market-entry strategy for a company billed as the world's first expert-level AI for marketers; went on to lead the website build, produce three how-to videos, plan weekly livestreams and write the brand workshop proposal.
  • Meet Taipei Startup Festival: worked closely with fintech start-ups, blockchain companies and accelerators, coordinating communication needs and advising on strategy.
  • Served as the Advertising department's first dedicated campus ambassador, connecting company and campus resources.

Takeaway

Connecting international finance companies with a local market brings both regulatory challenges and room for new models. This role clarified my positioning: not purely creative, not purely commercial, but the person in the middle who connects the two.
